## 1) **Game mechanics (TONE, REMNANTS, cascading logic)**

## 2) **Glyphs (70+ glyphs + fragments)**  
This is your symbolic backbone. It’s the part of Velinor that gives it identity, strangeness, and emotional resonance.

But glyphs are not gameplay by themselves.  
They are **meaning**, not **interaction**.

Glyphs become enticing when:

- the player decodes them  
- the player uses them  
- the player sees them mutate  
- the player sees them reflected in NPC behavior  
- the player gains mastery over them

Right now, glyphs are *rich*, but they are not yet *game verbs*.  
They are nouns waiting to become verbs.

Again: not a flaw. A stage.
