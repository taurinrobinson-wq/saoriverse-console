## Part 7: Emerging Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Emotional Exhaustion (Too Much Ache)

**Problem:** 15+ ache/collapse glyphs vs. only 4 joy glyphs = narrative exhaustion

**Mitigation:**
- Prioritize Tier 4 (Joy) simultaneously with Tier 1
- Distribute joy glyphs throughout exploration, not just at endgame
- Use joy as relief moments between heavy chapters

### Risk 2: Antagonist Feels Absent

**Problem:** If Drossel's glyphs aren't written, he remains a rumor, not a threat

**Mitigation:**
- Write Tier 2 (Drossel) as early as possible
- Give Drossel visible scenes in the marketplace before player meets him
- Make his opposition to Kaelen's arc a repeated, felt tension

### Risk 3: Narrative Inconsistency Across 50 Writers-Worth

**Problem:** If multiple writers work on glyphs, tone/quality could diverge

**Mitigation:**
- Create a **Glyph Writing Style Guide** (2-3 pages)
- Establish what makes a glyph narrative "work": structure, emotional beats, mechanical tie-in
- Review all Tier 1 glyphs before moving to Tier 2

### Risk 4: Scope Creep on Individual Glyphs

**Problem:** Each glyph could become a full quest; need to stay focused on emotional core

**Mitigation:**
- Each glyph should be a **single emotional moment**, not a multi-step quest chain
- Establish max length: ~300-500 words per glyph narrative
- Keep mechanics tied to glyphs, not expanding them into dungeons
