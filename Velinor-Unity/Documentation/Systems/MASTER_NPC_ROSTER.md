# Master NPC Roster – Velinor Settlement

A comprehensive catalog of named NPCs inhabiting the post-collapse settlement and surrounding territories. Each entry includes role, motivation, and thematic function within the narrative ecology.

---

## The Core Couple (The Emotional Foundation)

### Nima – The Guarded Bereaved
- **Role:** Market Keeper, Settlement Anchor
- **Defining Trait:** Armored by loss, holds the settlement market together through sheer grit and deep skepticism
- **Motivation:** Standing guard over her shattered home; protective of what remains
- **Narrative Function:** Emotional center; grounding point for player connection in Ravi/Nima marketplace scene
- **Story Arc:** Reveals true name and personal grief as dialogue progresses

### Ravi – Structural Sentry & Lookout
- **Role:** Safety Scout, Anguished Protector
- **Defining Trait:** Constantly tracking faultlines, tremors, and brittle masonry
- **Motivation:** Unresolved anguish of losing Ophina to a structural collapse
- **Narrative Function:** Grief as active responsibility; driving force for environmental safety measures
- **Story Arc:** Processing loss through protective vigilance; potential arc with Nima exploring shared grief

## Ideological Leaders & Lore Anchors

### High Seer Elenya – Prophet of the Great Silence
- **Role:** Spiritual Authority, Techno-Spiritualist Leader
- **Defining Trait:** Interprets shattered Corelink network as divine; treats relics as holy instruments
- **Motivation:** Finding meaning and structure in the collapse through spiritual framework
- **Narrative Function:** Represents the faith/spirituality camp in ideological conflicts
- **Canon Lore:** Leads the techno-spiritualist movement

### Archivist Malrik – Skeptic Chronicler
- **Role:** Materialist Ideologue, Knowledge Keeper
- **Defining Trait:** Insists remnants are inert scrap; rejects mystical links between artifacts and emotion
- **Motivation:** Pursuit of empirical truth; resistance to collective delusion
- **Narrative Function:** Ideological counterweight to Elenya; represents rationalism
- **Canon Lore:** Leads the materialist intellectual camp

### Sanor – Silent Recordkeeper
- **Role:** Data Chronicler, Malrik's Adherent
- **Defining Trait:** Silence as strict empiricism; records only physical, measurable data
- **Motivation:** Pure documentation; refusal to speculate or interpret
- **Narrative Function:** Living embodiment of cold rationalism; contrast to emotional characters
- **Notable:** His silence is philosophical choice, not disability

### Elka – Terminal Pilgrim
- **Role:** Techno-Spiritual Mystic, Corelink Seeker
- **Defining Trait:** Meditates before dying consoles; reads video snow, circuit hums, and error codes like scripture
- **Motivation:** Communion with the remnant network; searching for divine signal in digital noise
- **Narrative Function:** Represents the spiritual obsession with pre-collapse technology
- **Story Arc:** Potentially dangerous faith leading to risky artifact interaction

### Saori – The Faded Originator
- **Role:** Living Archive, Pre-Collapse Link
- **Defining Trait:** Quiet figure bound to pre-collapse era; presence carries deep resonance with network's original architecture
- **Motivation:** Carrying forward knowledge and memory of the "before times"
- **Narrative Function:** Bridge between old world and new; holder of crucial technical/historical knowledge
- **Story Arc:** Central to understanding the collapse; potential guide through digital spaces or archives

## Civic Order, Justice & The Underworld

### Captain Veynar – Settlement Guard Commander
- **Role:** Order Keeper, Perimeter Authority
- **Defining Trait:** Maintains discipline and regulates access between displaced survivors and scavenger bands
- **Motivation:** Stability through control; protecting settlement from external threats
- **Narrative Function:** Enforcer; can facilitate or obstruct player actions based on faction standing
- **Canon Relationship:** Works with Coren; in tension with Drossel

### Coren the Mediator – Civic Arbiter
- **Role:** Dispute Resolver, Justice Administrator
- **Defining Trait:** Settles boundary, debt, and salvage disputes before petty feuds escalate
- **Motivation:** Social cohesion; preventing violence through negotiation
- **Narrative Function:** Access point for learning settlement politics and faction dynamics
- **Canon Relationship:** Works with Veynar; respects across faction lines

### Drossel the Cloaked – Syndicate Boss (The Shadow Broker)
- **Role:** Underworld Leader, Black Market Operator
- **Defining Trait:** Runs protection rackets and back-alley runner networks; skims salvage from markets
- **Motivation:** Power and profit through shadow economy control
- **Narrative Function:** Darker faction option; access to illegal/rare goods and services
- **Canon Relationship:** In direct conflict with Veynar; employs lower-tier criminals

### Kaelen – The Fractured (Drifter / Swamp Trickster)
- **Role:** Dual-Identity Survivor, Unreliable Wanderer
- **Defining Trait:** Dissociative Identity Disorder manifesting as two distinct perceived personalities
- **Motivation:** Trauma fragmentation; coping mechanism through identity split
- **Personality A – The Drifter:**
  - Gentle, confused wanderer grasping for lost memories
  - Terrified of violence; seeks safety and belonging
  - Sympathetic but unreliable as quest companion
- **Personality B – The Swamp Trickster:**
  - Cunning marsh cutpurse; toys with patrols
  - Loots scrap cache sites; morally flexible
  - Chaotic but skilled at navigation and theft
- **Narrative Function:** Complex character exploring trauma and mental health; allows narrative branching on which "side" player sympathizes with
- **Story Arc:** Potential reconciliation or deepening fragmentation based on player choices

### Korrin the Gossip – Information Broker
- **Role:** Rumor Purveyor, Espionage Facilitator
- **Defining Trait:** Sits where foot traffic is heaviest; trades whispers, secrets, and faction movements for scrap
- **Motivation:** Information as currency; knowing everything about everyone
- **Narrative Function:** Quest giver; source of hidden lore and gossip about other NPCs
- **Canon Relationship:** Connected to all factions; neutral but expensive

## Crafts, Engineering & Practical Utility

### Thoran – Apparatus Tinker & Salvage Smith
- **Role:** Mechanic, Gear Crafter
- **Defining Trait:** Steampunk-style mechanic crafting scrap copper, lenses, and filters
- **Specialties:** Respirators, blast visors, utility rigs, complex mechanical devices
- **Motivation:** Engineering innovation from salvage; turning junk into tools
- **Narrative Function:** Source of technical gear upgrades and utility items
- **Canon Lore:** Likely one of the few artisans capable of advanced restoration

### Kiv – Master Stonemason & Monument Carver
- **Role:** Artisan, Grief Architect
- **Defining Trait:** Solitary carver eventually coaxed back into town
- **Story Arc:** Commissioned to chisel solemn memorial for young Ophina
- **Motivation:** Art as commemoration; transforming loss into permanent beauty
- **Narrative Function:** Connects to Ravi's grief arc; represents healing through creation
- **Canon Relationship:** Works with Ravi on Ophina memorial

### Seyla – Field Technician
- **Role:** Practical Engineer, Utility Specialist
- **Defining Trait:** Pragmatic; routes power lines and patches conduits without spiritual fanfare
- **Motivation:** Making things work; functional problem-solving
- **Narrative Function:** Contrasts with Elenya's mysticism; represents practical technology use
- **Canon Skills:** Power distribution, electrical systems, infrastructure repair

### Mariel the Weaver – Cordage & Textile Crafter
- **Role:** Essential Supplier, Canvas & Rope Producer
- **Defining Trait:** Makes heavy-duty materials for exploration and survival
- **Specialties:** Canvas, haulage lines, insulated wraps
- **Motivation:** Enabling exploration and survival; providing essential materials
- **Narrative Function:** Supplier for expedition gear; can provide quest items
- **Canon Relationship:** Works closely with scouts and wayfarers

### Lira – Skilled Boatmaker
- **Role:** Water Craft Builder, Canal Specialist
- **Defining Trait:** Builds shallow-draft skiffs suited for debris-choked environments
- **Specialties:** Navigation of canals, sunken vaults, water-based terrain
- **Motivation:** Enabling access to water-based locations; mastering aquatic spaces
- **Narrative Function:** Access point for water-based exploration and canal routes
- **Canon Locations:** Works near waterways; essential for flooded vault access

### Tovren the Cartwright – Heavy Wagonwright
- **Role:** Transport Engineer, Hauler Builder
- **Defining Trait:** Builds and repairs reinforced haulers
- **Specialties:** Moving massive machinery across cracked terrain
- **Motivation:** Enabling large-scale salvage operations; specialized logistics
- **Narrative Function:** Supports large expedition preparation; heavy-cargo specialist
- **Canon Relationship:** Works with salvage teams and major operations

## Medicine, Domestic Life & Survivors

### Helia – Triage Chemist & Distiller
- **Role:** Medical Authority, Alchemist Healer
- **Defining Trait:** Empirical apothecary using mineral broths and salves
- **Specialties:** Treatment of nerve tremors, broken bones, trauma medicine
- **Motivation:** Healing the collapse's physical wounds; medical care as lifeline
- **Narrative Function:** Quest giver for medicinal ingredient gathering; can sell remedies
- **Canon Lore:** Combines pre-collapse medical knowledge with survivor remedies

### Sera the Herb Novice – Field Forager
- **Role:** Apprentice Herbalist, Resource Gatherer
- **Defining Trait:** Scours damp perimeter ruins for resilient regrowth flora
- **Motivation:** Supporting Helia; learning medicine through practice
- **Narrative Function:** Quest partner; can guide to foraging locations
- **Canon Relationship:** Apprentice to Helia; developing expertise

### Tala – Market Cook
- **Role:** Morale Keeper, Sustenance Provider
- **Defining Trait:** Manages public cook-pots; transforms meager rations into hot food
- **Motivation:** Feeding people; morale through warmth and nourishment
- **Narrative Function:** Quest hub in marketplace; provides supplies and information
- **Canon Importance:** Sustenance as survival anchor; her food symbolizes hope

### Nordia the Mourning Singer – Bereaved Songstress
- **Role:** Grief Vocalist, Emotional Truth-Teller
- **Defining Trait:** Gives voice to unvarnished sorrow through laments
- **Story:** Lost family in the collapse
- **Motivation:** Processing grief through art; giving voice to collective loss
- **Narrative Function:** Atmospheric character; deepens emotional tone of settlement
- **Canon Style:** Raw, unadorned laments without ritual softening

### Tessa – Desert Widow
- **Role:** Isolated Survivor, Self-Reliant Loner
- **Defining Trait:** Weathered survivor living on arid outskirts
- **Motivation:** Persevering against grief through stubborn self-reliance
- **Narrative Function:** Optional quest giver; represents isolated survival paths
- **Canon Relationship:** Distant from settlement politics; self-sufficient

### Sealina – Street Performer
- **Role:** Morale Provider, Market Entertainer
- **Defining Trait:** Uses acrobatics and sleight-of-hand in market square
- **Motivation:** Bringing moments of levity to shell-shocked residents
- **Narrative Function:** Brings life and hope to marketplace; optional quest giver
- **Canon Skills:** Acrobatics, performance, sleight-of-hand (potentially useful skills)

### Inodora – Storyteller & Folk Chronicler
- **Role:** Oral Historian, Myth Keeper
- **Defining Trait:** Preserves myths and oral tales born in the vacuum of forgotten history
- **Motivation:** Preventing cultural erasure; keeping stories alive
- **Narrative Function:** Source of settlement lore, world history, and narrative context
- **Canon Importance:** Counterbalance to Malrik's cold documentation; keeper of meaning

## Surveyors, Scouts & Wayfarers

### Thalma – Outpost Provisioner & Logkeeper
- **Role:** Dry-Goods Supplier, Tremor Analyst
- **Defining Trait:** Shopkeeper whose rattling shelves inspired exact tremor logging
- **Motivation:** Survival through preparation; understanding environmental hazards
- **Narrative Function:** Quest giver; supplies for expeditions; source of tremor/structural knowledge
- **Canon Skill:** Can predict or detect structural instability through seismic patterns

### Lark – Canopy Scout & Overgrowth Tracker
- **Role:** Aerial Explorer, High-Canopy Specialist
- **Defining Trait:** Traverses aerial paths in high canopy where nature swallowed towers
- **Specialties:** Mapping overhead routes; canopy navigation
- **Motivation:** Discovering forgotten paths; exploring the "new wilderness"
- **Narrative Function:** Access to high-altitude locations; alternative route provider
- **Canon Terrain:** Expert in overgrown urban canopy and aerial terrain

### Dalen the Rusted Guide – Veteran Subterranean Scout
- **Role:** Underground Expedition Leader, Vault Specialist
- **Defining Trait:** Veteran guide through unstable underground vaults
- **Motivation:** Mastery of dangerous terrain; enabling deep salvage missions
- **Narrative Function:** Key companion for vault and underground exploration
- **Canon Expertise:** Structural instability detection; underground navigation

### Orvak – Ruined Watcher (High Sentry)
- **Role:** Lookout, Environmental Warning System
- **Defining Trait:** Keeps watch from broken spire; warns settlement of hazards
- **Specialties:** Dust storm warning, ruin shift detection
- **Motivation:** Protecting settlement through vigilance and early warning
- **Narrative Function:** Source of environment/weather information
- **Canon Locations:** High vantage point with settlement-wide visibility

### Sybil – Forest Loner Camper
- **Role:** Wilderness Hermit, Self-Sufficient Tracker
- **Defining Trait:** Chooses perils of wild woods over town politics
- **Motivation:** Freedom through isolation; independence from settlement drama
- **Narrative Function:** Optional quest giver; represents wilderness path; potential survival mentor
- **Canon Relationship:** Aloof from settlement; can appear in wilderness encounters

### Rasha – Ferry Operator
- **Role:** Transport Facilitator, Water Route Guardian
- **Defining Trait:** Poles heavy cable-barge across dangerous marsh basins
- **Motivation:** Safe transit enabled by skill and experience; fair exchange
- **Narrative Function:** Access point for marsh locations; toll-based travel system
- **Canon Terrain:** Master of dangerous water routes; essential for marsh access

### Juria & Korinth – Sea Merchants
- **Role:** Traders, Coastal Supply Line
- **Defining Trait:** Coastal traders supplying rare imported goods
- **Specialties:** Raw metals, dried fish, maritime salvage
- **Motivation:** Profit through trade; connecting settlement to wider world
- **Narrative Function:** Quest givers for rare items; represent broader world beyond settlement
- **Canon Relationship:** External traders; bring outside perspective and goods

## Canon Notes & Thematic Structure

### Ideological Spectrum
- **Spiritual Techno-Mystics:** Elenya, Elka, Saori (faith in remnant network)
- **Rationalist Skeptics:** Malrik, Sanor, Seyla (empiricism and data)
- **Pragmatists:** Thoran, Mariel, Thalma (function over philosophy)
- **Grief-Carriers:** Nima, Ravi, Nordia (processing through active engagement)

### Faction Allegiances
- **Settlement Authority:** Veynar, Coren, Thalma (official order)
- **Spiritual Movement:** Elenya, Elka, Saori, Inodora (faith and meaning)
- **Rationalist Collective:** Malrik, Sanor, Seyla (empiricism)
- **Shadow Economy:** Drossel, Kaelen (Swamp aspect), Korrin (information)
- **Neutral/Independent:** Sybil, Tessa, Rasha, Juria & Korinth (self-directed)

### Thematic Roles
- **Grief & Loss:** Nima, Ravi, Nordia, Inodora, Tala (processing collapse trauma)
- **Engineering & Reconstruction:** Thoran, Kiv, Seyla, Mariel, Tovren (building new world)
- **Exploration & Risk:** Lark, Dalen, Rasha, Sybil, Orvak (expanding boundaries)
- **Knowledge & Memory:** Malrik, Sanor, Elenya, Elka, Saori, Inodora, Korrin (preserving/interpreting)
- **Care & Sustenance:** Helia, Sera, Tala, Tessa, Sealina (life support)

### Key Relationships & Conflicts
- **Elenya ↔ Malrik:** Spiritual vs. Rational worldview (ideological opposition)
- **Nima ↔ Ravi:** Shared grief, mutual support (emotional anchor)
- **Veynar ↔ Drossel:** Order vs. Chaos (institutional conflict)
- **Ravi ↔ Kiv:** Grief → Memorial (processing through creation)
- **Saori ↔ Malrik:** Living history vs. Dead documentation (knowledge philosophy)
- **Kaelen (Drifter ↔ Trickster):** Internal conflict within single character

## Portrait Expression Framework

Each NPC should have approximately 9 emotional expression variations for dialogue system:
- **Neutral** – Default/calm state
- **Worried** – Concerned, anxious
- **Relieved** – Pleased, comfortable
- **Curious** – Questioning, interested
- **Touched** – Emotional, moved
- **Angry** – Frustrated, upset
- **Happy** – Joyful, content
- **Sad** – Sorrowful, disappointed
- **Confused** – Uncertain, bewildered

These expressions should reflect each character's emotional baseline and typical states.
