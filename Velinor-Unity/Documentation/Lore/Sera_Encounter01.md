SERA - The Herb Novice

&#x20;FIRST ENCOUNTER — MARKET COURTYARD

(Player approaches. Sera looks up from sorting herbs.)

SERA: “You look like you are searching for something.”

Development Notes: It is gentle, observational, safe.



*She gestures to the herbs*. “I come here when the shrine feels too quiet.”



Development Notes: She waits.  She does not push.  She lets the player choose how to enter the moment.





&#x20;TONE: TRUST

Player: “You seem comfortable here. I am glad I found you.”

SERA: *She relaxes slightly. Her shoulders drop.* “I am glad too. Most people hurry through the market. It feels nice when someone pauses.”

STATS CHANGE: Trust +.01, Resolve +.01, Skepticism -.01.

*She becomes a little more confident in the player’s presence.*



SERA: “If you ever need a quiet place, the shrine is open. I can show you around sometime.”



*This is the most forward she can be at a first meeting.*



TONE: OBSERVATION

Player: “You sort herbs here instead of at the shrine.”

SERA: *She brightens a little.*  “The air moves differently here. The herbs dry better. I do not know why. I just noticed it.”



STATS CHANGE: Nuance +.01, Memory +.01,  Authority -.01.

*She becomes more thoughtful, more perceptive, more deferential.*

She adds, “I like watching how things change when people pass by. It helps me understand the plants.”



TONE: NARRATIVE PRESENCE

Player: “You look like you are hiding from the crowd.”

SERA:  *She stiffens. Not offended — just suddenly self‑conscious.*  “I am not hiding. I am working. The market is simply… busy.”

STATS CHANGE: Authority +.01, Resolve +.01,  Nuance -.01.

*She becomes more literal, more formal, less emotionally textured.*

She adds, “I prefer to focus on the herbs. It keeps things simple.”



TONE: EMPATHY

Player: “It must be hard to feel small in a place like this.”

SERA: *She softens immediately. Her voice lowers.* “Sometimes it is. But the noise helps me feel more here. I do not know how to explain it.”

STATS CHANGE: Empathy +.01, Need +.01,  Resolve -.01.

*She becomes more vulnerable, more open, more dependent on the player’s gentleness.*

She adds, “Thank you for noticing. Most people do not.”



This is the deepest she can go in a first encounter without oversharing.



SHARED BEAT: *Sera offers a small sprig of herb.* “This one helps people feel steady. I do not know if it works. Sometimes I feel like it helps me remember where I am.”

She ends with: "You seem a lot more confident than I could ever be.”
