================================================================================
                            HOW IT WORKS:
================================================================================

BEFORE (Old Behavior):
  Scene A (Music Playing) 
      ↓ Scene Transition 
  Scene B (Music Stops) ❌

AFTER (New Behavior):
  Scene A (Music Playing: Glass Horizon)
      ↓ Scene Transition
  Scene B (Music Continues: Glass Horizon) ✅
      OR (Crossfades to new track if configured)
      OR (Fades to silence if requested)

KEY MECHANISM:
- Uses DontDestroyOnLoad() to survive scene unloads
- Singleton pattern ensures only one AudioManager exists
- Scenes can check IsMusicContinuing() and decide what to do

================================================================================
                            PUBLIC API:
================================================================================

QUERY METHODS:
  AudioManager.Instance                  - Get singleton instance
  AudioManager.Instance.IsPlaying()      - Check if music is playing
  AudioManager.Instance.IsMusicContinuing() - Check if continuing from prev scene
  AudioManager.Instance.GetCurrentTrack() - Get current audio clip
  AudioManager.Instance.GetMusicVolume()  - Get volume (0-1)

PLAYBACK CONTROL:
  AudioManager.Instance.PlayMusicTrack(clip, "Name") - Play immediately
  AudioManager.Instance.CrossfadeToTrack(clip, "Name") - Smooth transition
  AudioManager.Instance.StopMusic(duration) - Fade out and stop
  AudioManager.Instance.ResumeMusic() - Resume if paused
  AudioManager.Instance.SetMusicVolume(0.5f) - Set volume (0-1)

================================================================================
                            TECH DETAILS:
================================================================================

Singleton Pattern:
  - Prevents duplicate AudioManagers from being created
  - Only first instance is kept, others are destroyed
  - Accessible globally via AudioManager.Instance

DontDestroyOnLoad():
  - When SceneManager.LoadScene() is called, scene GameObjects are destroyed
  - DontDestroyOnLoad() marks an object to survive unload
  - AudioManager GameObject persists across scene transitions
  - Audio continues uninterrupted

Scene Transition Flow:
  Scene A loaded → Music starts playing
      ↓
  Player triggers scene transition
      ↓
  SceneManager.LoadScene("Scene B") called
      ↓
  Scene A GameObjects destroyed (except AudioManager)
      ↓
  Scene B loads
      ↓
  AudioManager.musicSource continues playing from before
      ↓
  Scene B Start() can check IsMusicContinuing()
      ↓
  Scene B decides: continue, change, or silence

================================================================================
                          TROUBLESHOOTING:
================================================================================

Problem: Music stops when scene transitions
Solution: Check "Persist Across Scenes" is ON in Inspector

Problem: Two instances of AudioManager exist
Solution: The singleton pattern prevents duplicates, but:
  - Ensure only one AudioManager in startup scene
  - Check no other scenes have AudioManager instances
  - Review scene loading code

Problem: Music doesn't play at all
Solution:
  - Verify AudioManager is enabled in Inspector
  - Check audio clips are assigned
  - Ensure AudioListener exists in scene
  - Check Time.timeScale isn't 0

Problem: Audio crackling during crossfade
Solution: Increase crossfadeDuration in Inspector (try 2-3 seconds)

Problem: Music volume too low/high
Solution: Adjust musicVolume in Inspector or use SetMusicVolume()
